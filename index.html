Essential switches & heating

Two things to know in the en-suite

If the shower or fan isn't working, the main switch is almost always the reason.

En-suite main switch

  1. Find the wall-mounted isolation switch outside or near the bathroom door.
  2. Make sure it's switched ON.
  3. This powers the shower and the extractor fan.

Bathroom radiator

Use the thermostatic valve on the bottom side of the radiator.

Clockwise ↻Warmer
Anticlockwise ↺Cooler or off
Shower not starting? Check the main switch outside the bathroom is on before anything else.
